Privacy policy

Your habit data should stay close to you.

StreakForge is designed to keep habit tracking private and lightweight. This page explains what data the app uses, what stays on device, and where Apple services are involved.

Summary

StreakForge stores your habits, check-ins, reminder preferences, and weekly review data locally on your device. The app does not require you to create an account and does not use a custom analytics backend for the core experience.

Information stored in the app

  • Habit names, descriptions, colors, icons, and schedules.
  • Check-in history and streak-related records.
  • Reminder settings such as enabled status and selected time.
  • Basic local preferences such as onboarding completion.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, StreakForge schedules local reminders on your device using Apple’s notification system. Those reminders are used only to help you return to your selected habits.

Purchases

Pro upgrades are handled through Apple’s StoreKit and the App Store. Purchase processing, billing, subscription management, and restore behavior are handled by Apple under Apple’s own policies and systems.

Data sharing

StreakForge does not sell your personal data. The app is intended to minimize what leaves the device, with Apple services involved only where necessary for notifications and App Store purchases.
